## Phase 1 findings investigated

- **Caption errors:** slide/authority matching supports COBIT, ISO/IEC 27001, HIPAA, hot-site and phishing spellings. Normalizations are logged; uncertain speech remains unresolved. PHI's Protected expansion is an explicit external correction.
- **Risk scales:** NIST distinguishes representative-number scales from quantitative measurements. Proposed correction is recorded; no silent repair or blanket approval of risk-owner/residual-risk assertions.
- **Recovery assumptions:** authoritative references distinguish recovery objectives and site readiness, and contradict the cold-site no-infrastructure wording. Fixed timing/loss guarantees and actual recovery capability remain held.
- **Metrics:** implementation/completion differs from outcome effectiveness; MTTR retains the source's Respond meaning. Timing boundaries and reporting-volume causality remain unresolved.
- **Legal claims:** HHS provides scoped contingency duties; EDPB supports risk-appropriate security. The full GDPR statutory page remained inaccessible behind robot checks; universal mandate/applicability claims are not approved.
- **Scorecard:** <10% in T:L111 versus unitless <10/12 in S36 remains an open conflict. No numerical interpretation was chosen.
- **AI:** official context is verified, but no course-derived AI knowledge was fabricated. Supplemental guidance/terms remain separate from the canonical course map.
- **Attribution:** candidate video retrieval failed; exact authorship/publication/rights and correspondence to the local recording are unverified. S20 exclusion is resolved only as a curation decision.

See [academic issues and gaps](academic-issues-gap-register.md) for each issue's evidence, proposed resolution, remaining uncertainty and future-question impact.
