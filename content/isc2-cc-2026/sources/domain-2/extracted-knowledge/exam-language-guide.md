# Domain 2 - Exam Language Guide

**20 reading-language records.** This guide describes source mnemonics, editorial interpretation and existing project guidance separately. It does not claim to reproduce live/official exam wording, predict question frequency, or provide question stems, options or answer keys. Thai is draft, with the qualifier's logical force preserved.

The course's explicit exam hints are the alignment commentary at T:L9-L13 and memory-aid recap at T:L113/S38. Other definition-based cues below are editorial synthesis. BEST/FIRST/negative-qualifier guidance comes from the existing [project authoring guidelines](../../../../../docs/plans/isc2-cc-2026/question-writing-guidelines.md), not from this lecture and not an official ISC2 template. The [canonical map](knowledge-map.md) controls conceptual limits; [issues](academic-issues-gap-register.md) control holds.

## Qualifier and mnemonic safeguards

No term is an answer shortcut independent of context. In particular, RPO does not automatically equal a backup interval, MTTR is not silently changed to repair/recovery, compliance does not establish every legal obligation, and a dashboard may contain a scorecard. More phishing reports do not prove either improved security or increased exposure without supporting evidence. Do not normalize the S36 vulnerability unit to make a future calculation possible.

**ไทย:** ใช้คำช่วยอ่านร่วมกับบริบทและหลักฐาน ไม่ใช้เป็นตารางจับคู่คำตอบสำเร็จรูป ข้อขัดแย้งเรื่องหน่วย นิยามเวลา และข้อกำหนดกฎหมายต้องผ่านการตรวจสอบก่อนนำไปใช้ประเมินความรู้
