### 1. The 9-Point Authoring Quality Gate Criteria

Every individual draft was audited against the following nine rigorous psychometric and academic standards:

1. **Defensible Single Best Answer:** Exactly one option among four is defensibly the BEST answer under authoritative standards.
2. **Answer-Explanation Concordance:** The explicit rationale directly supports the designated correct option index without contradiction.
3. **Plausible Distractors:** All three incorrect options represent authentic cybersecurity concepts, common real-world errors, or adjacent standards—never absurd or trivial throwaway choices.
4. **Context Completeness:** Scenarios provide all necessary operational, organizational, and regulatory parameters without ambiguity or missing facts.
5. **Strict Bilingual Equivalence:** English and Thai texts maintain semantic equivalence, ensuring the English question is fully self-sufficient and the Thai translation mirrors all nuances.
6. **No Unsupported Absolute Claims:** Avoids dogmatic assertions ("never", "always", "guarantees 100% security") unless mandated by technical definition.
7. **Zero Quarantined Content Leakage:** Strict quarantine enforced; 0% reference to blocked entries `KNOW-D2-028` and `KNOW-D2-054`.
8. **No Test-Taking Cues:** Options are balanced in length, grammatical structure, and tone, avoiding superficial cues like length biases or giveaway keywords.
9. **Semantic Independence:** Assesses distinct cognitive reasoning without duplicative overlap against Domain 1 or legacy question banks.
