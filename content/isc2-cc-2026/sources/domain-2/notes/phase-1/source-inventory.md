## Scope and results

Recursively enumerated every file, including hidden files, under `ISC2 Update 2026/Domain 2/`. **39 files, 13,805,705 bytes**: one TXT transcript (29,748 bytes) and 38 PNGs (13,775,957 bytes). No PDF, PPT/PPTX, DOC/DOCX, separate course notes, audio/video, archive or other supporting document was present in that directory. These absent formats are observations, not proof that a complete course must include them.

Read the full transcript and visually opened every PNG individually. Of 38 inspected PNGs, **37 are course captures and one is an unrelated product listing (S20)**. No file was inaccessible or wholly unreadable; cropped regions and overlays remain limitations. Counts of files/captures must not be reported as unique deck pages. Four repeated captures are identified in [cross-reference](source-cross-reference.md); 37 course captures represent approximately 33 distinct visible course views.

Source IDs `T` / `D2-T01` and `S01-S38` / `D2-Snn` are **local audit references**, not additions to the existing archive manifest. S-order is filename capture order, not verified deck numbering or video timing. T:Lnn is the physical one-based line in the unchanged transcript, including blank lines. Filenames' local capture times do not establish recording times or timezone.

A similar web video surfaced in discovery, but was not authenticated as this exact source and is not adopted as provenance. S20 is an unrelated incidental capture, not a Domain 2 reference.

No sources were copied into archive transcripts/slides and no old manifest, README or source was edited.
