# Domain 2 - Slide and image analysis

Prepared: 2026-10-23 (Asia/Bangkok). Phase 1 only; audit/editorial findings, not approved knowledge or question content.

## Review method and limits

All **38 PNG files were opened individually with image inspection** at their supplied resolution. There are 37 readable instructional captures and one readable unrelated image. None required guessing invisible text. All diagrams, tables and instructional illustrations are described below; decorative artwork and player UI are identified as such. Text behind a crop/overlay is **NEEDS_REVIEW** unless independently visible in a companion capture; companion evidence never repairs the original file. No OCR transcript or image was rewritten.

A 'confirmed' observation below means visible source support, not academic approval. Topic alignment is independently checked in [outline coverage](exam-outline-coverage.md). T:L references are topic matches, not exact frame/time alignments. [Inventory](source-inventory.md) supplies dimensions, exact sizes, classification and fingerprints. Every heading links to the original image.

## Sequence and duplication assessment

Source progression is GRC (S01-S10), resilience (S11-S19), awareness (S21-S27) and effectiveness (S28-S38). S20 is unrelated.
