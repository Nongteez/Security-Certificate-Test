## 1. Domain Overview & Verified Objectives

This domain focuses on the identity lifecycle, access controls, privileges, and authorization models that protect organizational systems and assets.

### Official Learning Objectives
- **Objective 3.1:** Understand identity life cycle management (Roles definition, provisioning, access review & recertification, deprovisioning & account revocation, SSO, federated identity, directory services)
- **Objective 3.2:** Understand logical access controls (Principle of Least Privilege [PoLP], Separation of Duties [SoD], access control models: DAC, MAC, RBAC, ABAC)
