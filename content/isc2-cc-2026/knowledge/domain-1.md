## 1. Domain Overview & Verified Objectives

This domain establishes foundational cybersecurity concepts, risk management, governance, security controls, and professional ethics.

### Official Learning Objectives
- **Objective 1.1:** Understand cybersecurity concepts (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, AAA, Non-repudiation, Privacy)
- **Objective 1.2:** Understand risk management concepts (Risk lifecycle, identification, assessment, response, monitoring)
- **Objective 1.3:** Understand governance concepts (Regulations, laws, frameworks, policies, standards, procedures)
- **Objective 1.4:** Understand cybersecurity controls (Technical/logical, administrative/managerial, physical)
- **Objective 1.5:** Maintain professional and ethical conduct (Code of conduct, due care, due diligence, ISC2 Code of Ethics)
