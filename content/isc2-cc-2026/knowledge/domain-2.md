## 1. Domain Overview & Verified Objectives

This domain covers organizational governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), redundancy, business continuity (BC), disaster recovery (DR), security awareness culture, and cybersecurity metrics.

### Official Learning Objectives
- **Objective 2.1:** Plan Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) (Purpose of GRC, business alignment, frameworks like NIST CSF and COBIT)
- **Objective 2.2:** Understand redundancy (Business Continuity concepts, Disaster Recovery, RTO, RPO, alternate processing sites)
- **Objective 2.3:** Understand security awareness (Organizational culture, tone at the top, social engineering, password hygiene, phishing forms)
- **Objective 2.4:** Measure cybersecurity effectiveness (KPIs, KRIs, dashboards, scorecards, executive reporting)
