## Scope and evidence limits / ขอบเขตและข้อจำกัด

The September 2026 names used here are **Domain 1: Security Principles (24%)** and **Domain 2: Security Governance (17.3%)**. Domain 2 includes GRC, redundancy/BC/DR, awareness, and measuring effectiveness; it is not the previous BC/DR/IR-only domain. The coordinating audit verifies the official outline separately. A course statement that a topic is “frequently examined” is not evidence of exam frequency.

All 24 Domain 1 numbered reading subsections, all 28 Domain 2 numbered subsections, and the review subsections are represented below. Reading metadata and both reusable scenario datasets were inspected. The 8 D1 and 12 D2 scenarios already exist in the Markdown and reusable JS datasets; that is existing content, not a gap for twenty new examples. Some initial large tool reads were truncated; the material used for specific findings was subsequently read in targeted ranges. Domain 2 review text was also inspected in its scenario dataset and source master.

Both original transcripts were read in full in bounded chunks. All eight original source Markdown files were enumerated and machine loaded; heading/topic inventories and relevant passages were inspected, but this is **not a completed sentence-by-sentence independent technical validation of every repeated paragraph in the long Example/Surm compilations**. Likewise all 20 published lesson files were inventoried at slide-ID/title/renderer level with selected explanation blocks inspected; this report does not claim full bilingual line-by-line review of every slide block or a rendered-browser QA pass. These limits matter: recommendations can be approved as a planning shortlist, but comprehensive source/bilingual acceptance remains a follow-up, not a claimed result.

All **98 original standalone PNGs** (60 D1, 38 D2) were actually viewed through 12 labelled contact sheets. This supports visual-type and obvious-content inspection; small body text and annotations are not all independently legible at contact-sheet scale. Source-embedded images that point to these same filenames were covered by that inventory. Two D1 screenshots are essentially blank/dark frames. The D2 `201852` screenshot is a small non-slide list whose details were not transcribed or verified. No external embedded images were downloaded; reuse rights were not cleared. The PNG index at the end makes the examined set explicit.

Classification is the primary action, not a demand that every subsection receive an addition. `KEEP EXISTING` and `DUPLICATE — DO NOT ADD` are positive decisions. Reuse/cross-link existing learning content before authoring another visual. Thai explanations should answer **คืออะไร → ใช้ทำไม → เกิดอะไรขึ้นจริง → ขอบเขต/ข้อจำกัด**; English terms remain visible, with matching meaning rather than word-for-word translation.

- Actual exam items or statements about what the exam “always” asks; unsupported frequency rankings; advanced cryptographic mathematics, PKI implementation labs, detailed ISO/COBIT/CMMC control IDs, legal retention schedules, vendor GRC deployment, production recovery/failover steps: **OUT OF SCOPE** for this enrichment audit.

Do not create another broad AI chapter just because source summaries are older. Link existing applications to the principle; avoid claims that a signature proves model correctness or that a checksum prevents poisoning.
- Live phishing/credential capture, sensitive real screenshots, production organization risk data and identifiable employee details are not needed. Rights/privacy approval is required before republishing any source screenshot; a local source file does not establish reuse permission.
