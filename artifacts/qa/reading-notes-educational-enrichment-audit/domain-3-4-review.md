Audit date: 2026-11-03. Planning/evidence only; no production edits or commit. Paths below are repository-relative; `R3` and `R4` mean `content/isc2-cc-2026/reading/domain-3.md` and `domain-4.md`. ## Outcome and scope

The strongest improvement is to bring Reading Notes up to the qualified explanations already present in published Learning Slides. The notes already have eight semantic diagrams, numerous comparison tables and 24 review scenarios. Creating another IAM lifecycle, four-model matrix, OSI ladder, DMZ picture, defense-in-depth onion or cloud responsibility stack would largely repeat existing instruction. Favor a short local explanation and a link to the existing lesson, with a static example only where independent reading otherwise fails.

The parent audit verified the live September 2026 outline (`research-isc2-outline.txt`): D3 is Identity and Access Management (IAM) Concepts, 20%, objectives 3.1/3.2; D4 is Networking and Cloud Security Concepts, 21.3%, objectives 4.1/4.2/4.3. The locally numbered 3.1.1–3.2.8 and 4.1.1–4.3.6 are editorial subdivisions, not additional official numbered objectives. AI integration guidance adds foundational applications, not an excuse for advanced product configuration or a separate invented objective. D3 metadata's `shortTitle: Access Controls` and D4's `shortTitle: Network Security` are legacy labels; title fields use the current names. Comment headers also retain old domain names. Treat this as navigation/editorial consistency, not evidence that old physical-access coverage must return.
