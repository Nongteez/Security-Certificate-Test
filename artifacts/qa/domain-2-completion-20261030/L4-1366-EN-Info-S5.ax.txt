    - generic: "An on-site copy can help local recovery but shares physical risks with the primary site. Cloud backups or off-site storage can reduce that shared exposure when copies, access and dependencies are appropriately separated. Location alone is insufficient: protect access and keys, identify a known-good point, restore data and configurations, then validate integrity and business use. Replication may copy corruption and is not automatically a historical backup. The official Domain 2 AI guidance adds that configurations and datasets powering AI services also need recovery planning; copying ordinary business data alone may omit those service dependencies. Test recoverability against RTO and RPO."
