  - paragraph:
    - generic: MTTD means Mean Time to Detect. This course uses MTTR as Mean Time to Respond; other organizations use the acronym differently, so explicitly define it before comparing values. For a local measure, define the incident start, detection and response milestones, then average comparable durations across a stated set of incidents. An average can hide a severe outlier and depends on what was observed or missed. Do not silently redefine response as complete recovery. The source also applies metrics to vulnerability remediation, awareness completion and patching requirements; each needs its own population, timeframe and interpretation.
