  - paragraph:
    - generic: "Business Impact Analysis (BIA) identifies important business functions, the impact of interruption over time and the resources each function depends on. These findings inform business recovery priorities in the BCP and the supporting IT restoration priorities in a DRP. Priority is based on business consequences and dependencies, not a universal three-tier label or the most expensive server. A lower-profile identity or network service may need restoration before a high-priority application can work. State assumptions, acceptable temporary service levels and resource constraints, and review them with accountable owners. As an official 2026 ISC2 supplement, model drift can also be a BC/DR risk: declining AI performance may disrupt a critical process. Identify the service dependency, monitor evidence and follow approved fallback/recovery procedures; drift does not automatically mean a cyberattack."
