  - paragraph:
    - generic: A Hot Site has the greatest pre-positioned IT readiness and usually higher standing cost. A Warm Site provides partial infrastructure but still needs preparation such as current data and configurations. A Cold Site mainly provides space and basic utilities; equipment and systems still need to be provisioned. These are relative models, not promised recovery times. Lecture ranges such as minutes/hours or days/weeks are illustrative. Network routing, identity, licensing, staffing, capacity and usable data all affect actual restoration. Select a configuration that demonstrates the required recovery objectives in exercises; the site name alone cannot prove a one-hour RTO.
