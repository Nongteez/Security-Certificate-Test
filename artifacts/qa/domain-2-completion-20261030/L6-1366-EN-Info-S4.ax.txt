    - generic: When a request is suspicious, stop the requested action and verify through contact details already known to be genuine, such as the organizational directory or help-desk portal. Do not use a number supplied by the suspicious message as proof. Follow the internal reporting process; if you clicked or disclosed information, say what happened promptly so responsible teams can assess containment and credential recovery. Do not investigate the attacker or improvise destructive cleanup. Official Domain 2 AI guidance includes tools assisting early identification/reporting and initial triage under established playbooks. An AI summary is assistance, not verified authority or permission to upload sensitive evidence to an unapproved service.
