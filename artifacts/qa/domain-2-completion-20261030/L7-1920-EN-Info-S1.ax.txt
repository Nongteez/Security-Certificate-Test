The source gives seven purposes: assess controls, find weaknesses, track performance, support risk-based decisions, demonstrate compliance, communicate to management and identify improvements. General examples include the number of incidents, number of vulnerabilities, percentage of systems patched, Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) and Mean Time to Respond (MTTR). A useful metric should be relevant, measurable, consistent and meaningful to its audience. Start with the business question, identify suitable evidence and decide who will respond; collecting more numbers alone does not establish effectiveness."
