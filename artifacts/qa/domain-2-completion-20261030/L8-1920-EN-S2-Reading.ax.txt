      - generic: "A security scorecard summarizes performance against predefined objectives or targets. It helps compare actual performance, track progress, identify concerns, communicate to management and support accountability. In the course example, patch compliance has a target of 95% and actual 91%, so it needs improvement against that target: a gap of 4 percentage points, not 4% relative change. These are illustrative organizational values, not ISC 2 requirements or universal thresholds. The target and actual must refer to the same measure, period and population; show the owner and follow-up so a status can lead to action. A scorecard may use colours but should also provide explicit text. Meeting one target does not establish that every critical asset is protected or that residual risk is acceptable."
