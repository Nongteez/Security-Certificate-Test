  - paragraph:
    - generic: Social Engineering uses psychological manipulation or deception to make a person act or reveal information. The source identifies Trust, Fear, Urgency, Authority, Greed and Helpfulness. An attacker may combine them, for example pretending to be IT and urgently requesting a password to prevent an account closure. Focus on the requested action and whether it follows an authorized process, rather than trusting a familiar name or a plausible story. A legitimate request can also be urgent, so the safe response is independent verification and appropriate reporting, not guessing from one cue.
  - list:

    - generic: A claimed title does not establish authority.
