  - paragraph:
    - generic: "Disaster Recovery (DR) combines policies, tools and procedures to restore IT infrastructure, systems and data after a disaster or major disruption. A Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) records who may activate recovery, what to restore first, the procedures and how teams communicate. Restoring a server is only part of recovery: identity, network access, application configuration, data integrity and usable business transactions must also be checked. DR supports Business Continuity (BC), whose broader responsibility includes people, facilities, suppliers and workarounds. BC and DR are capabilities; BCP and DRP are their documented plans."
