    - paragraph:
      - generic: This authored scenario combines an IT-support pretext with voice delivery, authority and urgency. It is vishing and pretexting; it does not become legitimate because the voice is familiar or the account has MFA. The supported response is to stop, avoid disclosing secrets, verify through the genuine help desk and report according to the organizational playbook. Use an independently known contact rather than one supplied by the caller. If you already shared a secret or approved a prompt, explain that promptly so the responsible team can assess account recovery. The goal is a safer decision, not identifying the voice technology or investigating the caller yourself.
