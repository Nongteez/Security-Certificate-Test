  - paragraph:
    - generic: Business Continuity keeps critical operations going across people, procedures, facilities, suppliers and technology. Disaster Recovery restores affected IT systems and data as part of that broader effort. For example, customer-support staff may work from a safe branch using an approved manual queue while technical teams restore the order system and validate its database. These activities can run in parallel; DR does not have to wait until every business workaround ends. BIA informs priorities and dependencies for both. The full comparison below preserves focus, scope, examples, planning documents, BIA use, recovery metrics and the relationship between BC and DR.
