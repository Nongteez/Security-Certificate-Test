    - generic: "For the 17:00 outage, an RPO of 30 minutes requires usable data from 16:30 or later; an RTO of one hour sets an 18:00 restoration deadline. These timestamps are targets, not observations that a backup or restoration actually occurred. The lecture gives two separate observed data-age examples with hourly backups: a 16:30 crash after a 16:00 backup loses 30 minutes; a later 17:20 crash after a 17:00 backup loses 20 minutes. Neither example supplies a service-restoration timestamp, so neither establishes an observed RTO breach. To evaluate a real result, check the last usable data point and the actual service-restoration time separately."
