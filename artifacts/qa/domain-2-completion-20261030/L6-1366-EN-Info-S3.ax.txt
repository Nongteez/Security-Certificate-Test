Pretexting describes the fabricated situation, such as an alleged IT emergency, used to obtain information or persuade someone to act. A single call can therefore be both vishing and pretexting. The official Domain 2 AI guidance adds AI-escalated social engineering and phishing: convincing generated messages or imitated voices make appearance and familiarity insufficient checks. Employees do not need to diagnose whether AI was used. Verify the request through known organizational channels and report the concern using the established process."
