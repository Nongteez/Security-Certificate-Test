  - paragraph:
    - generic: Awareness and training overlap, but serve useful teaching distinctions. A timely reminder can help a person recognize a suspicious message; practical training can teach how to use the reporting mechanism and what to do if they already clicked. NIST SP 800-50r1 supports ongoing awareness and learning appropriate to job responsibilities. Neither an annual attendance record nor a quiz result proves that safe behaviour is consistently supported at work. Use understandable reminders, relevant practice and feedback together. Do not treat awareness as only a one-time event or training as a replacement for working safeguards.
