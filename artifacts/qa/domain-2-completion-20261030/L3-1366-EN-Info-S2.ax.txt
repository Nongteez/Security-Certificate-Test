    - generic: "Course slide S12 identifies eight disruption examples: natural disasters, cyberattacks, power outages, equipment failures, network outages, pandemics, loss of facilities and supply-chain disruptions. The spoken lecture additionally mentions human error, such as a configuration mistake. These are examples to prepare for, not an exhaustive official threat taxonomy or mutually exclusive groups. One flood can close a building, interrupt power and prevent staff travel at the same time. A ransomware incident may require approved manual operations while security and recovery teams work on the systems. Choose a response that addresses the affected service and constraints; an alternate site is useful only if it is safe, reachable, connected and staffed. A plan must be exercised rather than assumed to work."
