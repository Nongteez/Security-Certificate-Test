    - generic: An observed metric does not explain its own cause. More phishing reports may indicate more suspicious messages, better awareness, easier reporting or a changed reporting scope. Confirm the definitions and look for corroborating evidence before declaring risk higher or lower. Leading indicators concern conditions preceding an outcome; lagging indicators summarize outcomes already observed. This distinction is supplementary context, not a rule equating every KRI with a leading measure and every KPI with a lagging one. A metric’s risk or performance role depends on the question it supports. Use uncertainty and data quality as part of the interpretation rather than hide them in a single green status.
