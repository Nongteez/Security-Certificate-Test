    - paragraph:
      - generic: Regular testing of the DR plan checks whether recovery arrangements still work. The DRP should document objectives, backup/recovery strategies, recovery sites and testing, supported by named responsibilities, authorization, contacts and dependency-aware procedures. Tabletop walkthroughs can reveal coordination gaps; restoration and failover exercises test technical recovery. Record the last usable data point and the time a validated service returns, then compare observed results with RPO and RTO. Assign owners to shortcomings, update procedures, train staff and retest after material changes. Coordinate with incident containment to avoid restoring compromised states, and communicate business priorities and service status through agreed channels.
