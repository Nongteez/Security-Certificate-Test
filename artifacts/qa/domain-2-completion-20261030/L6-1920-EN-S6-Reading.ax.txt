      - generic: "The original password-protection slide assigns organizations five responsibilities: authentication policies, stored-credential protection, appropriate access controls, suspicious-authentication monitoring and phishing-resistant authentication where appropriate. These complement employee habits. For example, a policy identifies approved authentication and recovery methods; access controls limit who can reach credential stores; monitoring gives responders evidence about concerning sign-ins. A user cannot compensate for an exposed organizational credential store by choosing a unique password alone. Technical owners must implement protection appropriately and avoid storing retrievable plaintext passwords. Authentication success also does not automatically authorize access to every resource."
