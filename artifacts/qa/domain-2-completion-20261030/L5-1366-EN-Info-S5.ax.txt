    - generic: A learning program should reinforce safe behaviour as work and threats change. Simulations should be authorized learning opportunities with constructive feedback, rather than a public ranking of people who made mistakes. Completion rates show participation, not whether a control works or people feel able to report. Look at context and practical outcomes; a rise in reports can reflect improved awareness as well as changing threat activity.
