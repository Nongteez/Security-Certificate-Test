    - generic: "MFA uses distinct authentication factors and can reduce the harm of a stolen password, but not every MFA method resists phishing. A manually entered OTP can be relayed through a fake site. NIST describes phishing resistance through cryptographic binding; FIDO2/WebAuthn provides verifier-name binding. Use approved authentication appropriate to the risk and protect recovery paths. Even phishing-resistant authentication does not prove that every request from an authenticated account is authorized or safe: account misuse, endpoint compromise and social engineering of non-login actions still need controls."
