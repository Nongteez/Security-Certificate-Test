slide.blocks.2.text EN: A §38 and B §6.2 describe deterrence as discouraging unwanted behaviour. A visible guard, camera or warning can signal scrutiny and consequences. In this authored facility example, the intended outcome of a visible camera is that a person decides not to attempt an unauthorised entry.
