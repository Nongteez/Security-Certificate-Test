EN: Source B says to collect personal data only as needed for the purpose. In this authored delivery example an address and an order reference support dispatch; unrelated health history does not. Data minimisation means choosing relevant, necessary information, not collecting every available field and relying on encryption later. Purpose limitation asks whether proposed use fits the specified purpose and applicable requirements. Explain the handling clearly to individuals and limit access to appropriate recipients. Consent is not the only possible legal basis in every jurisdiction or activity, and a checkbox does not justify unlimited collection or reuse.
