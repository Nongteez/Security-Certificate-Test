EN: Source B explains likelihood and impact and cautions that assessment methods vary. For the clinic, exposure and a relevant exploitable weakness may affect likelihood, while service dependence and workarounds affect impact. A low-likelihood event can still deserve attention when consequences are severe. Do not treat an unpatched-server count, a vulnerability severity score or a single red label as the complete risk judgment. This two-axis picture is conceptual, with no measured probability, score or official grading matrix.
