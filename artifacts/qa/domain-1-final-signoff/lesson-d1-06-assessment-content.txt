slide.blocks.2.text EN: Source A §21 uses SLE $5,000 and ARO 2 to obtain ALE $10,000 per year. The units explain the calculation: dollars per event multiplied by events per year gives dollars per year. ALE is expected annual loss under the stated simplified model; it is not the guaranteed amount lost every year, a maximum possible loss or the cost of a safeguard. Actual annual outcomes can differ. If repeat events involve repair or replacement, annual losses can exceed the value of one asset; make that scenario assumption clear rather than comparing incompatible scopes.
