slide.blocks.2.text EN: A §36 uses four course labels: technical, managerial, operational and physical. B §6.1 instead groups administrative/managerial, technical/logical and physical; the official 2026 Objective 1.4 lists technical, administrative and physical. Managerial and operational are useful supporting labels, not a fourth official category to add to the outline. In this lesson, policy/planning and people-led recurring processes are discussed within the administrative perspective.
