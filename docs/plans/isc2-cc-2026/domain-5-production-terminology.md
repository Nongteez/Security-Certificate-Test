## Decision-language controls

BEST/MOST appropriate require decisive constraints; FIRST/INITIAL/NEXT require current stage, authority and prerequisites; PRIMARY/MAIN require one dominant purpose; MOST likely preserves uncertainty; LEAST is avoided unless needed. No phrase creates difficulty by itself. English/Thai qualifiers and option ordering must agree. Abbreviations are introduced where useful. Thai retains English security terms without silently expanding assurance.
