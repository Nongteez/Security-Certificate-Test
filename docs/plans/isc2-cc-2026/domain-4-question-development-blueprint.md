## Scope and traceability

The dated ISC2 outline defines 4.1-4.3, not a count of questions or within-domain topic weights. AI applications come from its separate Domain 4 webpage commentary. Every target follows **official parent objective → original evidence or identified gap → externally verified KNOW record → target**. R IDs/sections are resolved in the register and cited knowledge entries. A target with an unresolved claim is excluded rather than granted readiness by a schema check.

Format labels indicate cognitive work: conceptual discrimination, scenario application, best-answer reasoning, control selection, and bounded troubleshooting/interpretation. Suitable scenarios describe teaching contexts and decisions; none is a candidate question. Plausible misconceptions are concepts to investigate later, not composed distractor options.

## Priorities and authoring controls

Prioritize distinct applied decisions about permitted paths, wireless/Bluetooth trust, constrained-device changes and cloud duty allocation. Use protocol/model recall to support interpretation rather than create a bank dominated by isolated port/layer facts. Include the official AI context at foundational level; no within-domain AI weighting is published here. FaaS/multi-provider extensions are optional supporting context, not a compulsory question quota.

Every future candidate must state enough conditions for one defensible best answer. FIRST requires a process stage, authority and relevant policy rather than a universal operational sequence. Plausible misconceptions need technically differentiated options; do not manufacture keyword/length/absolute clues. Review correct and every incorrect rationale, then independently compare English/Thai qualifiers, negation, scenario facts and option correspondence. Balanced keys alone do not establish psychometric validity.

The existing 86 updated items and all 583 legacy items remain semantic comparison targets. Particular overlap risks remain D1 Q601/Q605 (control classification/layering), Q589/Q593/Q607 (cloud privacy/risk/due diligence), D2 Q627/Q645 (metrics/reporting), Q636/Q643 (residual risk/password reuse), and D3 Q664/Q668 (contextual authorization/anomalous sign-in). Different names or wording do not make the same decision unique. There are no new candidates, so no duplicate-pass claim is made.

Exclude F02b/F02d/F10c and exact rate-table comparisons from authoring dependence. Product defaults, legal restrictions, costs, plant procedures and version-specific features need additional primary support if introduced. Vocabulary development remains separate after question development. This blueprint authorizes no drafting, data import, ID reservation or runtime change.
