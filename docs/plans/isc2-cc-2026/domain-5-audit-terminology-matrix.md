# Domain 5 question-bank audit — Examination terminology (D)

Date: 2026-11-03. This is an audit artifact, **not an update to the Vocabulary module**. [Source codes](domain-5-audit-source-inventory.md), [official mapping](domain-5-audit-objective-coverage.md), [R references / findings](domain-5-audit-academic-findings.md).

English terminology should remain visible to Thai learners. Thai descriptions below are explanatory, not a claim of an official ISC2 Thai translation. Confidence concerns the stated introductory distinction, not native-language certification or question approval. **H/V** = high confidence with the indicated primary corroboration; **M/L** = medium, local teaching/reviewer synthesis, needs a claim-level primary reference before publication. **H/G** = verified external concept filling a local gap; curation still pending. Abbreviation “—” means none needed, not that a new acronym should be invented.

## Decision wording and bilingual control

These are authoring controls, not claims about ISC2's internal lexical rules.

| English qualifier | Intended decision | Thai rendering / review check | Reject when |
|---|---|---|---|
| BEST / MOST appropriate | Most suitable option under explicit constraints | เหมาะสมที่สุด / ต้องรักษาเงื่อนไขที่ทำให้เหนือกว่าข้ออื่น | Several options meet the same goal equally well |
| FIRST / INITIAL | Earliest justified action at a specified starting state | อันดับแรก / เริ่มต้น; ระบุบทบาทและขั้นตอนที่ทำไปแล้ว | Reporting, containment and evidence preservation could reasonably occur together |
| NEXT | Immediate justified step after described completed actions | ขั้นต่อไป; อย่าเปลี่ยนเป็นอันดับแรก | No preceding action or governing procedure is provided |
| PRIMARY / MAIN | Principal purpose, cause or responsibility | หลัก / สำคัญหลัก; ไม่ใช่เลือกข้อที่ยาวที่สุด | Several benefits are equal and the stem does not constrain purpose |
| LEAST | Minimum suitability among the specified set | น้อยที่สุด; รักษาทิศทางคำปฏิเสธในไทย | Requires double negation or a vague “least secure” ranking |
| MOST likely | Evidence-supported inference with uncertainty | มีแนวโน้มมากที่สุด; อย่าแปลว่าแน่นอน | Attribution requires facts that are not supplied |

Use canonical spelling **SIEM**, **MITRE ATT&CK**, **SAST**, **DAST**, **patch**, **phishing** and **HIPAA** where relevant; do not copy automatic transcript artifacts. Do not add stand-alone identification/authentication, general threat/vulnerability, or governance taxonomy items merely because they appeared in the request's examples. Their use here is limited to operational context. Thai prompt/option field policy remains an open compatibility decision, while English stems/options and bilingual explanations are the requested authoring surface.
