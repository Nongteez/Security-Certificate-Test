A hash may check departure from a trusted baseline; it cannot certify the baseline's quality or fairness. Link to the corrected L01/L03 evidence limits.

Explain why a log or service-key signature is not automatic proof of human intent. Do not expand into a Domain3 lifecycle administration tutorial.

Connect risk and applicable duties to authorized acceptance, inquiry before/after use, transparency and potentially unfair results. Do not suggest a universal legal waiver or promise that human review guarantees non-bias.
