# Domain 5 question-bank audit — Assessment blueprint (E)

Date: 2026-11-03. **Planning only: no stems, options, answer keys or production questions are generated here.** [Scope](domain-5-audit-objective-coverage.md), [evidence](domain-5-audit-source-inventory.md), [findings and R references](domain-5-audit-academic-findings.md), [terminology](domain-5-audit-terminology-matrix.md).

## Recommendation and rationale

Propose **50 distinct accepted items**, provisionally, across 26 assessment-opportunity families. Counts arise from separate concepts/decisions in the table, not a domain-to-domain quota, slide count or an estimate of confidential exam frequency. Each comma-separated cognitive target below represents one distinct item slot; two slots in one family must measure different listed decisions. Merge/drop a slot if duplication review shows no added value. Do not pad the bank to reach 50.

This is a **conditional sufficiency target**, not a promise of immediate readiness: AT06 and AT14 require new curated knowledge; all affected findings must be resolved before drafting. At most 46 slots are grounded without those four gap-dependent slots, and even those require normal claim review. Extra retries or rejected drafts do not count toward the target.

## Cognitive design

Categories are local assessment-design labels, not ISC2's internal taxonomy. A = foundational understanding; B = conceptual differentiation; C = practical application; D = scenario interpretation; E = decision-making under explicit conditions. Difficulty is separate: introductory understanding may still use a short scenario. No empirical difficulty can be claimed until learner data exists.

## Sufficiency and overlap controls

All fourteen official bullet groups have at least one opportunity family; the four handling examples are not hidden under cryptography, and all three testing groups are separately represented. Quantum and IR-data-policy gaps receive explicit held slots. AI cases replace a suitable AT02/AT10/AT14 context after curation; they do not inflate the count.

The diversity comes from distinct decisions: category versus indicator, limited display, irrecoverability, key role, trusted comparison, quantum risk, collection, triage, signal interpretation, correlation, motivation, intelligence use, framework purpose, governed evidence, authorized response, restoration, exercising, lifecycle, unsupported assets, configuration state, governed change, team collaboration, test evidence, code/runtime methods, design risks and physical verification.

Cross-domain overlap watch list: Q603 backup as corrective control; Q613 retirement as risk avoidance; Q620 restore-testing evidence; Q626 employee incident reporting; Q624 support impersonation; Q668 anomalous-login validation; Q690 change path validation; Q702 AI development/production controls. Preserve every existing item. A D5 item may use the same setting only if it tests a demonstrably different operational decision. Compare with **all 702 existing questions** (583 legacy +119 updated), not only the four updated domains. Embedded local-source quizzes are study evidence, not approved item templates.

## Volume versus exam assembly

Do not equate a 50-item Domain 5 practice bank with its draw in one mock exam. Current normalized largest-remainder allocation for 125 questions gives Domain 5 **21** in this application; the official 17.3% is an average domain weight, not a guarantee of 21 questions on an ISC2 adaptive exam. The application is a fixed weighted sampler, not validated CAT. Current incomplete bank returns 96 of 125 requested items; future D5 would fill 21 of the missing 29, while Domain 1's 24-item pool remains below its 30-item allocation and Domain 3's 23-item pool below its 25-item allocation. With 50 D5 items and unchanged D1–4, the current engine would supply **117/125**, not 125. This is an integration limitation to report, not permission to modify D1–4 or the engine.

Approve/revise this plan only after the evidence gaps and overlap judgments are reviewed. Next authorized task should be **knowledge curation and findings resolution**, not immediate generation of 50 questions.
