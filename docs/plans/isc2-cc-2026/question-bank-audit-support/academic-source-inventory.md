## Contradictions, provenance and research holds

- `D1 sum.md:280` lists location alongside factors; `Domain 1.md:102` correctly lists three factor categories. Location/device context must not be authored as a fourth independent authentication factor. Current [NIST SP 800-63-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63.html) distinguishes knowledge, possession and inherence.
- `D2 sum.md:486` expands MTTR as respond but supplies no operational endpoint. Course framing cannot justify the universal containment endpoint asserted by Q629. Define a metric before calculation. [NIST measurement guide](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-55v1.pdf) uses multiple time measures and warns that numbers can mislead without context.
- D2 course numeric 1–4 risk matrices are not automatically quantitative; ordinal scoring may be semi-quantitative. Transcript spelling such as HIPPA is not terminology authority.
- D3 original transcript equates identity/username and credentials/username-password and simplifies provisioning order. Corrected `domain-3-knowledge-map.md:53` onward explicitly records those corrections; use the corrected distinction with current NIST identity guidance. Do not author from the unqualified transcript alone.
- D4 transcript line 85 describes WPA3 as almost impossible to break. Reject that assurance claim. `D4 surm.md` supplies qualified networking notes; no wireless standard or version guarantees every implementation.
- D5 transcript lines 53–57 use signing/encrypting-with-private-key shorthand. `D5 sum.md:142` correctly distinguishes signature creation/verification from confidentiality; [NIST signature guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/Digital-Signatures) supports the distinction. Do not generalize RSA-style shorthand to all signatures.
- `D5 sum.md` opening labels 5.1 as cryptography; official 5.1 also covers classification, labeling, masking and sanitization. Other local summaries contain extra topics; neither heading placement nor supplemental material creates an official objective.
- Several expanded notes refer to PDFs supplied in an earlier conversation, including the August 2026 update article. No such PDF is present in the five mandatory directories; referenced content is not treated as an inspected local PDF. The official outline was verified online.
- D1 staging notes still show 26% and combined legacy counts; D2 staging still references proposed 1001+ IDs. They are historical staging records, not current runtime counts. D3 prior review overstates that balanced keys completely prevent guessing bias; key balance cannot prove item quality.
- Legal applicability, exact retention/erasure duties, contested control taxonomy, universal MTTR definitions, guaranteed RPO-zero designs, advanced cloud/AI implementation and exact cryptography transition rules require primary-source research if a future item depends on them. None is invented here.
- Local expanded notes identify conversation-derived and supplemental material; similar text in multiple files is not independent corroboration. Embedded local practice examples are learning aids and overlap candidates, never a pool to copy into the bank.

The newly inspected D5 transcript passage `DOMAIN 5.txt:7–29` supplies additional 5.1 support for classification, labeling, masking and sanitization, beyond the cryptography summary heading. Its claims need qualification: employee-only Confidential is an example scheme rather than a universal classification; masking does not make data incapable of misuse; the last four card digits alone do not establish strong authentication; and cryptographic erase requires suitable prior encryption and all relevant recoverable key copies to be addressed, not simply encrypt-then-delete. Current Q1001/1002 appropriately supply policy context, and Q1006 explicitly tests a remaining escrow key; do not import the transcript simplifications into new explanations. Detailed media-specific sanitization procedures require current primary guidance, not assumptions based on SSD/cloud labels.

The inspected [official ISC2 ethics page](https://www.isc2.org/ethics) identifies four mandatory canons and the need for professional judgment; it does not establish that word-order override. Reject that instructor shortcut, and do not build an item keyed to violating law because another word appears earlier. The proposed canon contexts use the qualified local summary and official conduct principles instead.
