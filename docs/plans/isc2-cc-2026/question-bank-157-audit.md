## Method, evidence and limits

The real `js/core/question-loader.js` loaded **157 unique questions**, from the five `data/certs/isc2/cc/versions/next-bank/questions/domain-N.js` modules. The audit reviewed all English stems/options and structured correct/incorrect rationales, mapped all questions, measured option/stem patterns, and screened bilingual field/letter structure. This was a single-agent desk review with the intended keys visible, **not an independent blind answer-key review, psychometric validation, or native Thai sign-off**. Thai semantic checks were targeted, not a complete line-by-line bilingual certification. The per-item register explicitly leaves that gate open for every item. No browser validation was needed or performed for this documentation-only phase.

Local sources were recursively inventoried before question analysis. See [academic-source-inventory.md](question-bank-audit-support/academic-source-inventory.md), including **all 277 file paths**, 29 indexed text files, section-level supporting evidence and six newly inspected images. Text academic inspection was targeted; the remaining 242 images and unreviewed paragraphs are not silently declared accurate or complete. These are material review limits, especially for later question generation.
