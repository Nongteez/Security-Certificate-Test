## Disposition

The materials provide substantial introductory operations and testing instruction, but are **not ready to use verbatim for question authoring**. There are 21 findings below: **9 High, 9 Medium, 3 Low**. High blocks affected concepts; Medium requires qualification or additional evidence; Low is a provenance/editorial limitation. No Critical finding is assigned because this task does not deploy controls or publish a bank.

“Verified correction” means a cited primary reference supports the proposed technical treatment, not that a human has approved the correction. Unresolved findings stay visible. All future items remain unauthored and unreviewed.

## Positive evidence worth preserving

N and S explicitly improve several original simplifications: signatures versus confidentiality (N106/S156), salted password hashing (N102/S128), contextual IOC interpretation (N346/S465), and the residual risk of compensating controls (N587/S754). They add meaningful explanations, but do not become independent authoritative sources. The transcript explains VPN-related false positives at T119–121. I36 contributes the priority matrix absent from a simple prose retelling. I14 preserves a public-value key-agreement process that summary text could flatten into generic encryption.
