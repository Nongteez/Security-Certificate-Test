- [ISC2 official 2026 outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc/cc-certification-exam-outline): Objective2.1 is **Plan Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)** — purpose, importance, frameworks and tools. Source lists are course teaching detail, not claims of fixed official schemas or actual exam frequency.
- [NIST SP800-30r1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final) §2.1.2 / AppendixI and [NIST assessment terminology](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/measurements-for-information-security/terminology): distinguish ordinal/semi-quantitative ranking from quantities with meaningful units/proportionality. Quantitative analysis is not restricted to money. A product of illustrative rank labels is a prioritization aid, not precise expected financial loss or probability.
- [NIST IR8286Ar1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8286/a/r1/final): risk scenarios connect assets, threats/vulnerabilities, likelihood/impact and registers that support response/monitoring. A register format varies; preserve all11 source fields without presenting them as a universal mandatory schema.
- [NIST inherent-risk definition](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/inherent_risk) and [residual-risk definition](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/residual_risk): state the baseline; residual risk is what remains after responses/controls, not qualitative subtraction or a universal numerical lower bound. Intended control effects require evidence; planned results are projections. Avoidance addresses the specified risky activity and can introduce other risks.
- [NIST SP800-39](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication800-39.pdf) §3.3: response selection is informed by risk criteria/tolerance; transfer/sharing allocates agreed consequences/responsibilities and does not automatically remove operational harm or retained organizational duties.
